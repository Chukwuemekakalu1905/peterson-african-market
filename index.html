Departments

Typical African market goods

01 · Rice & grains

Rice, millet, and flours used for fufu, garri, and everyday meals.

02 · Oils & condiments

Palm oil, groundnut oil, tomato paste, and bottled sauces.

03 · Spices

Peppers, dried fish seasonings, curry, and bouillon.

04 · Frozen case

Fish, goat, and other frozen proteins when available.

05 · Drinks

Malta, juices, and other bottled drinks.

06 · Snacks & staples

Biscuits, plantain chips, and packaged pantry goods.
